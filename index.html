I am a Postdoctoral Research Associate in the Department of Civil, Environmental, and Geo-Engineering at the University of Minnesota, Twin Cities. My research focuses on asphalt materials, reclaimed asphalt pavement (RAP), and pavement performance, combining laboratory testing, full-scale and field evaluation, finite element modeling, and data-driven methods.

At Minnesota, I study asphalt mixture compaction, low-temperature cracking, and long-term pavement performance in research projects funded by the Minnesota Department of Transportation (MnDOT), collaborating with researchers at Michigan State University, the Federal Highway Administration (FHWA), the National Center for Asphalt Technology (NCAT), and Università Politecnica delle Marche (Italy).

I received my Ph.D. from Tongji University in 2023, where my dissertation received the Outstanding Doctoral Dissertation Award, and spent a year as a visiting Ph.D. researcher at Politecnico di Milano with a China Scholarship Council fellowship. I received my B.E. from Chang’an University.

Research

My research links the behavior of asphalt materials to the performance of pavements in service. It spans laboratory characterization, full-scale accelerated pavement testing and MnROAD field evaluation, and finite element and data-driven modeling, with an emphasis on recycled materials and long-term durability.

01

Recycled and Sustainable Pavement Materials

Reclaimed asphalt pavement (RAP), rubberized asphalt as a rejuvenator, warm mix asphalt, and construction and demolition waste. This work covers the mix design and gyratory compaction of high-RAP mixtures, heat transfer in RAP during plant production, and recycled materials for airfield pavements.

Selected work

  • Thermal conductivity of reclaimed asphalt pavement and its constituentsRoad Materials and Pavement Design, 2026
  • Effect of crumb rubber and RAP on the viscoelastic property of asphalt mixtureJournal of Cleaner Production, 2023
  • Recent applications of waste solid materials in pavement engineeringWaste Management, 2020
02

Aging, Viscoelasticity, and Cracking

How coupled temperature, moisture, and ultraviolet radiation age asphalt binders and mixtures, and how aging changes their viscoelastic behavior and low-temperature cracking resistance. Methods include accelerated aging, binder rheology (DSR, BBR), and cracking tests (DENT, SCB).

Selected work

  • Low-temperature behavior of asphalt binders under field and laboratory oxidative ageingJournal of Materials in Civil Engineering, 2026 (in production)
  • Radiation-thermal-moisture coupling effects on the viscoelasticity of rejuvenated reclaimed asphaltConstruction and Building Materials, 2025
  • Moisture resistance of stone matrix asphalt under simulated high temperature and continuous rainfallInternational Journal of Pavement Engineering, 2022
03

Pavement Performance: Testing, Sensing, and Modeling

Full-scale accelerated pavement testing (APT) and MnROAD field evaluations, combined with embedded sensors, finite element modeling, and physics-based and data-driven analysis, including wavelet analysis, inverse heat analysis, and machine learning, to assess pavement condition and long-term performance.

Selected work

  • Performance of flexible, semi-flexible, and rigid pavements under accelerated pavement testingConstruction and Building Materials, 2023
  • Rutting and strain characteristics of rubberized asphalt pavement under accelerated pavement testingJournal of Cleaner Production, 2022
  • A two-stage feature reduction (FIRRE) framework for neural network predictions in civil engineeringInfrastructures, 2026

Methods

From material characterization in the laboratory to full-scale testing, field evaluation, and computational analysis.

  1. Laboratory

    Materials characterization

    • Binder rheology (DSR, BBR)
    • Mixture mechanical testing (UTM, MTS)
    • Accelerated aging, FTIR, GPC
    • Moisture susceptibility (HWT, ITT)
    • Low-temperature cracking (DENT, SCB, DT)

  2. Full scale & field

    Performance evaluation

    • Accelerated pavement testing (APT)
    • MnROAD field evaluation
    • Embedded sensor instrumentation and monitoring
    • Friction testing

  3. Computation & data

    Modeling and analysis

    • Finite element modeling
    • Artificial neural networks
    • Statistical modeling
    • Image processing
    • Large language modeling (BERTopic)
    • LiDAR data processing

Software: Python · MATLAB · FreeFEM++ · ParaView · SPSS
